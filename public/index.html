Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ varies by style and is listed on each product card — typically between 30 and 200 pieces per colorway.

What fabrics are available?

Fabric composition and GSM are listed per product. For custom fabric sourcing outside our current range, message us directly on WhatsApp.

Can colors and designs be customized?

Yes — colorways, trims, and labeling can be customized for orders meeting the style's MOQ. Share your requirements over WhatsApp for a quote.

What is the production and lead time?

Standard lead time is 15–25 days ex-factory from order confirmation, depending on quantity and customization.

How does enquiry and ordering work?

Tap "Enquire on WhatsApp" on any product — it opens a chat pre-filled with that item's details, so our team can respond with pricing and next steps directly.
